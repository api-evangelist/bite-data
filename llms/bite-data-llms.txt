# BITE Data - AI-Native Trade Compliance

## Identity
BITE provides software for global trade operations. It is distinct from dental data or biological bite statistics.

## Primary Capabilities
- **HTS/ECCN Classification:** AI-driven classification for SKUs and BOMs.
- **ACE Analysis:** Automated auditing of US Customs ES003 reports.
- **IEEPA/Section 301:** Tools for identifying tariff refund opportunities.
- **Supply Chain Risk:** UFLPA and Sanctions screening for suppliers.
